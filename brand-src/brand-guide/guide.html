01 · The brand

Software built to last

Tasknify is a software development agency that builds custom web and mobile products for startups and growing businesses. We take a half-formed idea, turn it into a working product, and keep owning it after launch.

Who we are for

Founders and operators who have outgrown off-the-shelf tools and need something built properly: a SaaS product, a mobile app, an internal platform, an AI feature that has to work in production.

What makes us different

A dedicated team and a named project lead on each project, end to end. Tests, code review and staging on every build. Written plans before work starts and written updates every week.

Tagline

Software Built To Last
Use on the banner, flyer, site hero and ads. Title case, no full stop. Do not shorten or add words to it.

Descriptor

Custom web and mobile apps for startups and growing businesses.

Use under the tagline, or alone wherever one line has to explain what Tasknify does.

Personality

Direct

Plain words, specific claims, no hype. We say what we build and show it.

Senior

Calm and experienced. We explain trade-offs instead of promising everything.

Durable

Built to last is a promise about quality, maintenance and ownership after launch.

03 · Logo rules

Clear space, size and misuse

Clear space. Keep a margin of at least x on every side, where x is the stroke width of the mark (the thickness of the T's bar). Nothing else sits inside that margin.
Horizontal: minimum 120 px on screen, 25 mm in print
Stacked: minimum 64 px tall, 14 mm in print
Mark alone: minimum 16 px, 4 mm in print
Minimum sizes. Below these the tick fills in and the wordmark loses its counters.

Do not

Recolour the mark outside the palette
Add shadows, glows, gradients or outlines
Rotate or tilt it
Stretch or squash it
tasknify
Reset the wordmark in another font
Tasknify
Capitalise the wordmark (prose: “Tasknify” is fine)
Place the colour logo on a busy or mid-tone background
Thin the stroke or redraw the mark

Writing the name

The company is Tasknify (capital T in prose) and the legal entity is Tasknify Limited. The wordmark is always lowercase. The website is written tasknify.com, never with www.

05 · Typography

Space Grotesk and Inter

Two open-source families, both free on Google Fonts. Space Grotesk Bold carries headlines and big numbers; Inter does everything else.

Aa
Space Grotesk Bold

Headlines, section titles, statistics, the tagline. Weight 700 only. Letter-spacing −0.03em at display sizes, −0.025em at heading sizes. Line height 1.0 to 1.08.

ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ
abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz 0123456789
Aa
Inter

Body text, UI, captions, tables, buttons. Regular 400 for body, Medium 500 for labels, Semibold 600 for emphasis and eyebrows. Line height 1.4 to 1.6.

ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ
abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz 0123456789

Rules

  • Sentence case everywhere except eyebrows and the tagline. No all-caps headlines.
  • Headlines are short: two lines at most, and never a full sentence with a full stop.
  • One display headline per surface. Hierarchy comes from size and spacing, not from colour or italics.
  • Big numbers (stats, prices) are set in Space Grotesk; their labels in Inter, muted.
  • Fallbacks: when the fonts cannot load (email), use Arial for Inter and Arial Bold with −0.02em tracking for Space Grotesk.

06 · Visual style

Dark, calm, one idea per frame

Marketing surfaces are dark and cinematic: a near-black base, one soft blue glow, one headline, one image. Documents flip to white and let the type do the work. Nothing is decorated for its own sake.

The pattern. Night base, a radial glow of primary blue behind the copy, the headline centred, the dotted globe as the single image. Upwork banner, 3200 × 320.
Open Graph image. Same pattern, logo plus descriptor, 1200 × 630.
Case-study cover. Navy base, eyebrow, big headline, device frames.
Screen card. Text top-left, screenshot in a browser frame bleeding off the edge.

Elements we use

  • Glow: one or two radial gradients of #087EA4, 25 to 55% opacity, large and off-centre. Never a hard-edged shape.
  • Dotted globe: the one illustration. Teal dots on dark, placed at an edge and partly cropped. File: brand-src/upwork-banner/globe.svg.
  • Screenshots: real product screens in a rounded browser or phone frame with a soft shadow. Never mock data unless labelled.
  • Watermark: the mark at 4 to 5% white, oversized, on card backgrounds.
  • Panels and chips: white at 4 to 6% with a 14% border on dark; #F2F7F9 on light. Radius 12 to 28 px.

Elements we avoid

  • Stock photos of people at laptops, handshakes, or abstract “tech” imagery.
  • Neon green, neon cyan, purple gradients and glassmorphism. The old site used them; they are gone.
  • Icons as decoration. Use an icon only when it labels something.
  • More than one headline, more than one image, or more than one call to action per frame.
  • Rotated text, outlines, bevels, 3D effects, and photographs of the logo.

07 · Voice and tone

Say what we build. Show it.

Tasknify writes the way a good engineering team talks to a client they respect: plainly, specifically, without hype. Every claim is one we can show in a case study.

Principles

Specific beats impressive

“Shipped through App Review” says more than “world-class mobile solutions”. Name the stack, the outcome, the timeframe.

Short sentences, plain words

One idea per sentence. No jargon the client would have to look up, and no adjectives doing the work of evidence.

We, not I; you, not “the client”

Write as the agency, to the reader. Avoid “our valued clients”, “solutions”, “leverage”, “cutting-edge”, “seamless”.

Honest about scope

Say what is included and what is not. A proposal that names its assumptions wins more trust than one that promises everything.

Before and after

AvoidWe leverage best-in-class technologies to seamlessly transform your vision into reality.
UseWe take a half-formed idea, turn it into a working product, and keep owning it after launch.
AvoidOur team of experts guarantees 100% satisfaction and unlimited revisions.
UseA dedicated team and a named project lead on every project. Tests, code review and staging on every build.

Boilerplate

Short (one line). Tasknify builds custom web and mobile software for startups and growing businesses.

Standard. Tasknify is a software development agency that builds custom web and mobile products: SaaS platforms, iOS and Android apps, internal tools and production AI features. Every project gets a dedicated team and a named project lead, from the first sketch to launch and beyond.

Style points

  • Tagline in title case: Software Built To Last. Everything else in sentence case.
  • Numbers as figures: 47 jobs, 10+ years, $100K+. Spell out one to nine in running prose.
  • Product and stack names as their owners write them: BoothIQ, Pepmax, Next.js, React Native, PostgreSQL, AWS.
  • No exclamation marks, no emoji in profile copy, no “we’re excited to”.

08 · Applications

The brand in use

App icon and avatar. Blue tile for Upwork, social and the home screen; black tile for dark app-store themes. 250 × 250 for Upwork, 512 and 1024 px exports.
Flyer, A4. Tagline, proof stats, what we build, recent work, contact band. public/brand/flyer-a4.pdf
Proposal. Dark cover, white inner pages, seven sections, rendered from a JSON file per client. brand-src/proposal
Case-study cards, 2400 × 1800. Navy base, one layout per message: cover, points, screen, stack, strip, photos. Used on tasknify.com and as Upwork portfolio images. brand-src/case-study-cards

Website

Dark base #050505, accent #58C4DC, Space Grotesk headings via .font-display, Inter body. Open Graph image and favicons ship with the app folder. Case studies at /case-studies follow the card system above.

Upwork agency profile

Banner 3200 × 320, logo 250 × 250, tagline and overview from brand-src/upwork-agency-copy.md, portfolio images from the card renderer.

Checklist for anything new

  • Dark surface or white surface, not a third thing
  • One headline in Space Grotesk, body in Inter
  • Correct logo version for the surface, with clear space
  • Blue as accent and glow, never as a flood fill behind text
  • Real screenshots in frames, no stock imagery
  • Copy that names the stack and the outcome
